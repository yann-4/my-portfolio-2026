// Origin story

About me.

I'm Yann, a Grade 12 student at Path To Success International School who wants to study computer engineering at university. I like knowing how things work, and then making them work better.

How it started

I grew up watching Iron Man. Tony Stark building a suit in a cave out of a box of scraps showed me that engineering is about solving problems with whatever you have, and making it look good.

My dad was the one who pushed me into tech. He saw that I wanted to build things and kept encouraging me. Now I'm working toward a career in computer engineering, one project at a time.

So far that has meant wiring sensors to an Arduino with my friends, setting up routers in the networking lab, running a music server in Docker, writing Python, and building this site in Django.

Model United Nations

I've taken part in Model United Nations, representing countries, researching global issues and debating resolutions with other delegates.

MUN taught me to speak with confidence, think on my feet and see a problem from more than one side. Those skills matter just as much in engineering, where you have to explain your ideas and work in a team.
